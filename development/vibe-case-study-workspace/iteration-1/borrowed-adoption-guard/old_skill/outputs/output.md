Meng’s case can inform the narrative pattern, but its adoption evidence cannot be used as evidence for your AI research summarizer. The repository can demonstrate that the product works, while 60 likes demonstrate launch interest. Neither proves reuse, teaching, invitations, redistribution, or independent use, so I would omit the Adoption block from the Compact case.

Use this evidence-safe replacement:

> **Launch interest**
> **60 likes on the launch post**
> The release attracted attention, but follow-on use has not yet been verified.

Keep the working product as a separate product proof—for example, a short input-to-summary demo—rather than presenting it as user impact. Only upgrade this to an **Adoption** block after collecting a direct receipt such as product analytics showing completed sessions or return use, an anonymized message from someone who used it independently, or evidence that someone shared or taught the workflow.
