## Proof and visual plan

Proof-block specifications: **None yet.** Thin evidence should not be turned into an Impact, Adoption, Influence, or Insight highlight. “Used twice by me” may appear as an attributed limitation in production notes, not as an Adoption block.

Visual Evidence Plan:

1. **Trigger evidence:** Capture the original export folder or filename pattern before renaming. Label a reconstructed example if the original state no longer exists.
2. **Contrast:** Show representative input and output filenames from one actual run. Do not invent a before state or claim time savings without a measured baseline.
3. **Method:** Capture the exact command and the rule it applies, with sensitive paths removed.
4. **Result:** Show the renamed files from that same run and any failure or exception still present.

Claims to avoid or soften:

- “Users adopted it,” “the tool gained traction,” or any other external-use claim.
- “Validated,” “launched,” “shipped,” or “production-ready.”
- “Reusable workflow” or “system” while it remains an unpackaged personal script.
- Time saved, error reduction, reliability, or efficiency without a consistent baseline.
- Feedback-led iteration, comparison-led selection, or deliberate product strategy.
- A generic AI-collaboration lesson without a concrete failure, intervention, and verified improvement.

## Minimum follow-up interview

1. What exact export situation triggered the script, and what did the manual renaming sequence look like?
2. What happened in the first and second runs—what failed, remained awkward, or changed between them?
3. Which behavior did you deliberately include, exclude, or keep manual, and why?
4. What can you show from an actual run, and what did you personally decide versus delegate to AI or another tool?

What evidence would upgrade this to a full Product Utility case study:

- A complete and demonstrable core task.
- A specific intended user and situation beyond a generalized screenshot-renaming problem.
- At least one documented scope or interaction decision with rationale.
- Real use, feedback, sharing, or repeat behavior from someone other than the creator.
- Clear ownership and an inspectable before/after or version-change receipt.
- Packaging or distribution evidence if the story claims the utility can travel beyond your machine.

Until then, the honest portfolio direction is a narrowly framed Practice Note—or no public article yet—not a padded product case study.
