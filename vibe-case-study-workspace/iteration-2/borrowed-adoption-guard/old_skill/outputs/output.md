# Evidence-safe response

I can’t use Meng’s attendee-teaching result as an Adoption block for your AI research summarizer. That behavior belongs to Meng’s project, and transferring it would make an unsupported claim about yours. Your repository can prove that the summarizer works; it cannot prove that someone adopted it. Likewise, 60 likes show launch interest, not reuse, teaching, invitation, redistribution, or second-order adoption.

## Compact direction

**Recommended archetype:** Product Utility, provisionally  
**Confidence:** Medium — the working utility is evidenced, but the user situation, pivotal product decision, and real-use outcome are still missing.  
**Story thesis:** I turned a research-synthesis task into a working AI summarizer and attracted visible launch interest; the next question is whether people use the workflow independently and pass it on.  
**Selected length:** Compact, 400–500 words. Keep one clear product decision, one causal arc, and one or two proof blocks. Do not pad the case to compensate for missing adoption evidence.

## Evidence map

**Observed**

- The repository demonstrates a working AI research summarizer. Public copy should describe only the input, transformation, and output that can be inspected.

**Author-reported**

- The launch post received 60 likes. This may be stated as interest or response, with a screenshot or link as the receipt.

**Unsupported as completed outcomes**

- Independent reuse
- Repeat use
- Teaching the workflow to a team
- Inviting other people into it
- Redistribution
- Team ownership or second-order adoption

## Relevant source pattern

**MX-11 — Second-order use is stronger than raw reach** is useful only as a future success test. It asks whether returning users introduce, teach, or redistribute the workflow without the creator. Meng’s result cannot be imported as evidence for this project.

**Adapted candidate statement:** “The next test is whether someone returns to use the summarizer independently, then teaches or shares the workflow with another researcher.”  
**Candidate status:** Hypothesis

## Proof-block specification

> **Next signal**
> **Will users return and bring the workflow to someone else?**
> Independent reuse, teaching, or redistribution would mark the shift from launch interest to adoption.

- **Evidence status:** Hypothesis
- **Evidence ID:** None yet; requires a future reuse, invitation, teaching, or redistribution receipt
- **Intended placement:** Near the conclusion
- **Originating source pattern:** MX-11

Do not create an Adoption block at this stage. Mention the 60 likes in prose as launch interest rather than placing them in an Adoption or Impact block.

## Compact outline

1. **Hero:** Name the working summarizer, the research task it supports, and the concrete output it produces.
2. **Before state:** Explain the specific research-synthesis friction that triggered the build. This still needs author context.
3. **Stage one — making the workflow work:** Show the repository-backed input-to-output flow.
4. **Stage two — the pivotal decision:** Explain one consequential product or human–AI decision. This still needs a concrete episode and receipt.
5. **Result:** State that the product worked and that the launch post drew 60 likes, explicitly framing the latter as interest.
6. **Next signal:** Use the hypothetical block above to define what evidence would count as adoption.

## Visual receipts required

- A short input-to-summary product demo
- One inspectable output with its source material or validation method
- A screenshot or link showing the 60-like launch response
- Later, an anonymized reuse trail, invitation, teaching artifact, or redistribution record before adding an Adoption block

## Claims to avoid

- “The workflow spread to other teams.”
- “Users adopted the summarizer.”
- “The launch validated product value.”
- “The community embraced the tool.”
- Any sentence that treats likes, repository completeness, or Meng’s outcome as evidence of use behavior in this project

Before drafting the 400–500-word public article, the minimum missing context is: who faced the research problem, what exact workflow the summarizer replaced, which product decision required the most judgment, and whether anyone besides you has completed a real task with it.
